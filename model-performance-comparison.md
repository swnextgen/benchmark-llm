- **C1:** single-request decode TPS / mean TTFT in seconds.
- **C4, C8 and C12:** aggregate TPS / mean per-request decode TPS / mean TTFT in seconds.

## Client-update summary

- **Highest interactive and per-user throughput:** Ornith-1.5-35B-A3B-NVFP4.
- **Highest C12 aggregate throughput and lowest TTFT:** Qwen3.6-35B-A3B-NVFP4.
- Performance represents this exact workload and configuration; model quality must be compared separately with the task benchmark.
